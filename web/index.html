Belgium Exam Rules

  • 50 Questions: You must answer 50 multiple choice questions.
  • Passing Limit: Minimum score required is 41 out of 50.
  • 15-Second Limit: You have exactly 15 seconds to answer each question once it is displayed.
  • Major Offences Penalty: Wrongly answering a question classified as a "Major Offence" (infraction grave) deducts 5 marks instead of 1. Making two major mistakes is an automatic fail!
